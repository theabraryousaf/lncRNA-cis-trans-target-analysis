# Manuscript methods text

Candidate cis- and trans-target genes of lncRNAs were predicted from significant lncRNA and protein-coding genes. Gene biotypes, gene symbols and genomic coordinates were assigned using GENCODE annotation after removing Ensembl version suffixes. Candidate cis-targets were defined as protein-coding genes located within 100 kb upstream or downstream of each lncRNA locus and were identified using BEDTools genomic interval analysis. Candidate trans-targets were inferred from expression co-variation across 0 h control and 16 h, 20 h and 24 h treated samples. Normalized counts were transformed as log2(count + 1), and Pearson correlation coefficients were calculated between each lncRNA and each protein-coding gene. Genes with no expression variation were excluded. Correlation P values were adjusted using the Benjamini–Hochberg method, and pairs with |PCC| ≥ 0.85 and FDR ≤ 0.01 were retained as candidate trans-targets. The original PCC sign was used to classify associations as positive or negative. Predicted lncRNA–mRNA relationships were considered candidate regulatory associations.
